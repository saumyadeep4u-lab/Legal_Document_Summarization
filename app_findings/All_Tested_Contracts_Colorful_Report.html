Customer shall complete system integration and initiate live operational deployment (the "Launch") within sixty (60) days following the Effective Date. 3. Following the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year periods (each a "Renewal Term") unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term. 4.3 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches any provision hereof and fails to cure such material breach within thirty (30) days following receipt of written notice detailing the breach. 4.4 Termination for Convenience. Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time after the first twelve (12) months by providing ninety (90) days prior written notice to Licensor, provided that no prepaid subscription fees shall be refunded. 5. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, neither party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, punitive, or exemplary damages, including loss of profits, revenue, data, business interruption, or goodwill, arising out of or in connection with this Agreement. 6.2 Cap on Liability. In no event shall Licensor’s total aggregate cumulative liability arising out of or related to this Agreement exceed the total fees actually paid by Customer to Licensor under this Agreement during the twelve (12) month period immediately preceding the event giving rise to liability. 7. CONFIDENTIALITY 7.1 Protection of Information. Each party agrees that all code, inventions, business, technical, and financial information disclosed to it by the other party constitute confidential property ("Confidential Information"). The receiving party shall hold such information in strict confidence and use reasonable care to prevent unauthorized disclosure. 8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION 8.1 Governing Law. MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AND PROPRIETARY INFORMATION AGREEMENT This Mutual Non-Disclosure and Proprietary Information Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of March 10, 2023 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Horizon Quantum Technologies Inc., a Delaware corporation located at 750 Innovators Way, Cambridge, MA 02142 ("Disclosing Party"), and Apex Cyber Defense Solutions LLC, a California limited liability company located at 350 Silicon Boulevard, San Jose, CA 95134 ("Receiving Party"). 1. PURPOSE OF DISCLOSURE The parties wish to explore a potential strategic business collaboration, technology integration, and joint product development initiative in the field of post-quantum cryptography and cloud orchestration (the "Transaction"). In connection with evaluating and pursuing the Transaction, each party may disclose to the other certain confidential and proprietary information. 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION 2.1 Scope. "Confidential Information" means all non-public, sensitive, or proprietary data, technical schematics, software source code, algorithms, customer lists, business strategies, pricing schedules, financial models, and invention disclosures disclosed by one party ("Discloser") to the other ("Recipient"), whether orally, visually, or in writing, and marked as "Confidential", "Proprietary", or which reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the circumstances of disclosure. 2.2 Exclusions. Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was already in the lawful possession of Recipient prior to disclosure without an obligation of confidentiality; (c) is independently developed by Recipient without reference to or reliance upon Discloser's information; or (d) is rightfully obtained from a third party authorized to make such disclosure. 3. OBLIGATIONS OF NON-DISCLOSURE AND NON-USE 3.1 Duty of Care. Recipient agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and take all reasonable precautions to safeguard it, applying at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own highly confidential information, but in no event less than a reasonable standard of care. 3.2 Restricted Use. Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating and carrying out the Transaction. Recipient will not reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble any hardware prototypes, software builds, or cryptographic binaries provided under this Agreement. 3.3 Need-to-Know Access. Recipient may disclose Confidential Information only to its officers, directors, employees, legal advisors, and outside financial consultants who need access to perform obligations under the Transaction and who are bound by written non-disclosure obligations no less restrictive than those herein. 4. TERM AND SURVIVAL 4.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall remain in effect for a period of two (2) years, unless terminated earlier by either party upon thirty (30) days prior written notice. 4.2 Survival. The obligations of non-disclosure and non-use with respect to all Confidential Information disclosed during the term shall survive termination or expiration for a period of five (5) years; provided, however, that with respect to any trade secrets and core source code, such obligations shall survive indefinitely until the underlying material enters the public domain without fault of Recipient. 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND INVENTIONS Nothing in this Agreement grants either party any ownership rights, title, patent licenses, or intellectual property rights in the other party's pre-existing software, models, or proprietary assets. All modifications, improvements, or derivative works resulting from the evaluation of Discloser's Confidential Information shall remain the exclusive property of Discloser. 6. REMEDIES AND ASSIGNMENT 6.1 Injunctive Relief. The parties acknowledge that unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information will cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate. Accordingly, Discloser shall be entitled to seek immediate injunctive relief and equitable remedies in any court of competent jurisdiction without the requirement of posting a bond. 6.2 Anti-Assignment. Neither party may assign, delegate, or transfer this Agreement or any rights or obligations hereunder, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of the other party. Any unauthorized assignment shall be null and void.

For a period of eighteen (18) months following separation, Executive shall not induce, solicit, or recruit any employee, contractor, key vendor, or active client of the Company to terminate or diminish their commercial relationship with the Company. 5. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts without giving effect to conflicts of law principles. Provider grants Subscriber a non-exclusive, non-sublicensable, subscription-based license to access and utilize Provider’s managed distributed database platform, real-time query infrastructure, and associated developer interfaces solely for Subscriber’s enterprise e-commerce applications. 1.2 Infrastructure Hosting. Provider shall host, operate, maintain, and upgrade the cloud platform in secure tier-3 data center facilities, ensuring redundant power feeds, automated geographic failover, daily snapshots, and compliance with SOC-2 Type II standards. 2. SERVICE LEVEL COMMITMENT AND REMEDIES 2.1 Uptime Guarantee. Provider commits to maintain a monthly system availability and service uptime of at least 99.95% ("Target Uptime"), measured twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week, excluding scheduled maintenance windows announced at least seventy-two (72) hours in advance. 2.2 Service Credits. If Provider fails to meet the Target Uptime during any calendar month, Subscriber shall be entitled to financial service credits calculated as follows: (a) 99.0% to 99.94% Uptime: 10% credit of monthly subscription fee; (b) 95.0% to 98.99% Uptime: 25% credit; (c) below 95.0% Uptime: 50% credit. Service credits shall be applied against subsequent invoices and represent Subscriber's sole monetary remedy for downtime. 3. FEES, PAYMENT TERMS, AND AUDIT RIGHTS 3.1 Platform Fees. Subscriber shall pay an annual base platform fee of $240,000, invoiced quarterly in advance in four equal installments of $60,000. Invoices shall be payable net forty-five (45) days from invoice date. 3.2 Over-Usage Charges. Query executions exceeding 50 million transactions per month shall incur overage charges of $0.002 per additional thousand queries, invoiced monthly in arrears. 3.3 Audit and Compliance Verification. Subscriber and its certified independent technology auditors shall have the right, up to once per calendar year upon thirty (30) days written notice, to audit Provider’s security logs, third-party penetration test summaries, and disaster recovery simulation records to ensure adherence to data security covenants and regulatory standards. 4. WARRANTIES AND LIMITATION OF LIABILITY 4.1 Performance Warranty. Provider warrants that the SaaS platform will operate without material operational bugs and in compliance with documentation under normal enterprise workloads. 4.2 Cap on Cumulative Liability. Except for gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of data privacy obligations, Provider’s maximum aggregate cumulative liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be strictly capped at the total amount paid by Subscriber in the six (6) months preceding the incident giving rise to liability. 4.3 Consequential Damages Exclusion. In no event shall either party be liable for any lost profits, lost data, loss of business reputation, indirect, special, or consequential damages arising under this Agreement. 5. TERM AND TERMINATION This Agreement shall remain in effect for an initial period of two (2) years and automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives written notice of termination at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the current term.
